🚀 HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS
The Magic Flow:

Employer deposits money → Paystack holds it → Funds go to company wallet
Employer adds employees → Bank details verified via Paystack
System schedules payroll → Bull queue job runs daily at midnight
9 AM on payday arrives → Bull job processes payroll:

Checks wallet balance
If sufficient: Creates Paystack bulk transfer
Deducts from wallet
Updates employee next payment dates
Sends notifications


If insufficient funds → Pauses payroll + Notifies admin to deposit
Webhooks confirm → Paystack sends real-time payment status updates
